Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

This page explains how Guomu (the iPhone app) and this website handle data.

Files and notes

The files you send to Guomu, along with their notes, statuses and versions, are stored in the 過目 (Guomu) folder in your own iCloud Drive, or on your iPhone if you are not signed in to iCloud. They are not sent to our servers.

Account

Guomu needs no account and does not ask for your name or email address.

When the app goes online

Only in these five cases:

  1. Post links: when you share a post's link, your phone asks that website directly for the page's public preview data (author, text, first image). The request does not pass through us.
  2. Reporting a problem: only when you tap Send, the app sends your description, the contact details you choose to give, the app version, the system version, the device model, the interface language, and a random number created at install time that is used only to limit how many reports can be sent per hour. These are stored in our Google spreadsheet and used to find the problem and reply to you.
  3. Update check: the app asks Apple's public lookup service for the current version number.
  4. Ads in the free version: the free version shows Google AdMob ads. Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and usage and diagnostic data for advertising (how Google uses this data). The app asks whether you allow tracking first; if you do not, every feature still works. Pro does not load ads.
  5. Purchases: Pro purchases are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the effective date above will be updated.

This website

This website (sona-orbit.github.io) uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read, and to count clicks on the App Store link. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives the pages you view, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. This applies only to the website. You can block it in your browser’s privacy settings or with Google’s opt-out add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Terms of use

Use of Guomu and purchases in it are covered by Apple's standard end user license agreement (EULA): www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
