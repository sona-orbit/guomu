Frequently asked questions

Guomu is not in the share sheet.

Scroll the row of apps in the share sheet all the way to the right, tap More, and add Guomu.

Why is the label wrong?

iOS does not tell Guomu which app shared the file. Guomu guesses from the link's domain or a tag the agent wrote into the file, and otherwise uses your last label. You can change it before you confirm.

Where are my files?

In the 過目 (Guomu) folder in your iCloud Drive. You can see it in the Files app on iPhone and on your Mac; the folders inside are your labels. Without iCloud, files stay on your iPhone.

Why does a post have only one image, or nothing at all?

Guomu reads the public preview data of the post's page, which has only the first image and no video. For X, the preview data only includes the author's profile picture. Posts that are not public or need a login cannot be read.

The free version is full at 10 deliveries.

Delete a few, or upgrade to Pro. Files you confirmed while it was full are not lost.

How do I restore a purchase?

In Guomu's settings, tap the line under Plan to open the Pro page, then tap Restore Purchases. The iPhone must be signed in to the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose Guomu and cancel. A one-time purchase needs no cancelling.

How do I report a problem?

In Guomu's settings, tap Report a Problem, describe what happened and send it. Leave a way to contact you if you want a reply.
